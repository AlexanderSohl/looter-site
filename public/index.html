Top Down Looter

A real-time action looter. Work in progress: the game updates as content is added.

You can play without an account. Log in below to save your progress online, use it on any device, and post scores to the leaderboard.

Your account

Optional. Without an account you can still play: your progress stays in this browser. An account saves your progress online so it follows you to any device, and puts your scores on the leaderboard under your name.

Names are 3-16 letters, numbers, spaces, - or _. Passwords are 8-72 characters. There is no password recovery yet, so pick one you will remember.

Logged in as

Your game save is backed up online a few seconds after each change. Log in with the same name and password on another device to continue there.

Change password

Changing your password logs out your other devices.
